Home Assistant Nest Integration

This is a personal, non-commercial application that connects a single self-hosted Home Assistant instance to Google's Smart Device Management API, for the sole purpose of allowing its owner to view and control their own Nest camera and thermostat from their own home automation system.

This app is not a commercial product, is not distributed to other users, and is not affiliated with Google or Nest.
